MEHYARSOFT — BUSINESS AI WORKFLOW CHECKLIST

1. Choose one task
What task repeats? Who does it? What causes delays or mistakes?
Record baseline volume and handling time before estimating savings.

2. Locate the knowledge
Which approved documents, policies or records should the system use?
Who owns them, updates them and approves an answer?
Do not put sensitive customer records into a public inquiry form.

3. Review the tools
List email, calendars, CRM, documents and other systems involved.
Check API access, permissions, vendor terms and integration costs.

4. Define human control
Which actions may be prepared as drafts? Which need explicit approval?
How should missing information, exceptions and urgent requests reach a person?

5. Agree scope and costs
Separate discovery, build, hosting, model usage, messaging and vendor costs.
Define ownership, acceptance checks, support and changes in the agreement.

6. Test before launch
Try normal cases, incomplete requests, conflicting information and denied access.
Check that the system follows approved information and escalation rules.
Never treat an illustrative demo as proof of measured business results.

7. Make the handoff clear
Name the operator. Document access, fallback, monitoring and maintenance.
Make sure staff can pause automation and handle requests manually.

8. Improve using evidence
Compare actual use with the baseline. Review errors and staff feedback.
Expand scope only after confirming the workflow is useful.
