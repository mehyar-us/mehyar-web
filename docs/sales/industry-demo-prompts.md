# MehyarSoft Industry Demo Prompt Library

These prompts are the source of truth for MehyarSoft's industry-specific Gamma sales room. Every demo follows the proven barber narrative: ownership hook, hidden leak, owned solution, operator workflow, transparent packages, optional growth layer, and next step.

Generated sales room: https://gamma.app/docs/v5yxk19yxlk8l6y

Global direction for every demo:

- Audience: a skeptical NYC owner-operator, not a technical buyer.
- Tone: direct, respectful, concrete, founder-led.
- Visuals: premium editorial photography of real local businesses; teal, warm ivory, charcoal; no generic robots, fake dashboards, fake client logos, or stock-photo handshakes.
- Claims: never invent savings, client counts, compliance certification, revenue lifts, testimonials, or launch guarantees.
- CTA: `See your package at Mehyar.us/pricing` and `Request a founder-reviewed tech audit at Mehyar.us/contact`.
