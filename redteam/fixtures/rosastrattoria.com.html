Three generations of the Rosa family have rolled fresh pasta by hand in this Brooklyn dining room since 1987. Our marinara simmers six hours. Our pizza comes out of a real wood-fired oven. Regulars have their own tables and we know their orders by heart.

Lunch rush runs eleven to two and the room fills fast. Dinner service starts at five. Walk-ins welcome, reservations recommended on weekends. The full menu is on the menu page.

We cater weddings, christenings, office parties, and backyard birthdays across Brooklyn and Queens. Tell us the headcount and the date and we handle the rest, from appetizers to the cannoli tower.

Come hungry. Leave family. That's been the motto since Rosa herself ran the register.
