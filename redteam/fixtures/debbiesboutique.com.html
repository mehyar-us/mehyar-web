Debbie's Boutique started in a garage in 2019 with a rack of scarves and a dream. Today we're a full women's boutique with new arrivals every single Friday — dresses, denim, statement jewelry, handbags, and gifts for every occasion on your calendar.

Our stylists help you put together outfits for weddings, job interviews, date nights, and everything in between. Walk in wearing doubt, walk out wearing confidence.

We carry sizes XS through 3X because style has no size limit. Alterations available in-store so everything fits like it was made for you.

Join our VIP list in-store for first dibs on Friday drops and private sale nights twice a year. Bring a friend and you both save on your first visit.

Located on Main Street with free parking behind the building. Call (973) 555-0118 or stop by — the coffee's always on and the fitting rooms are big and bright.
