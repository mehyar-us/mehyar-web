Footer carries all shared legal links (see compliance section). Viewport, OG/Twitter tags, consent-gated GA4 all present. Minor: no `rel=canonical` on `index.html`.
- **`/sales` — FAIL.** Pricing section contains ZERO dollar figures (grep confirms 0 `$` characters in `sales.html`) while promising "No hidden fees." Tier names (Start/Grow/Top-up) mismatch the app (Free/Pro + credit packs). No link anywhere to `/pricing` — the only page with real prices. Seven trade cards link to nothing (vertical pages are orphaned). No Twilio/Telnyx provider-billing disclosure on the page.
- **`/pricing` — PASS (minor).** Shows `Free $0`, `Pro $14 / month`, top-ups `Small $4`, `Medium $8`, `Large $12` one-time, and "No hidden fees · No credit card to start · Cancel anytime · Purchases are final — no refunds." Footer legal links present. Minor: missing Open Graph/Twitter meta tags (sales.html and all vertical pages have them).
- **`/get-started` — PASS (with note).** Returns HTTP 200 but renders the generic app landing via SPA fallback — there is no dedicated get-started marketing page. No marketing CTA on any page points to `/get-started` (all CTAs point to `/`). So "deep links work" = no 404, but the route is contentless.
- **Vertical pages (`/sales/salon`, `/sales/restaurant`, `/sales/plumbing-hvac`, `/sales/dental`, `/sales/auto-repair`, `/sales/pet-grooming`, `/sales/med-spa`) — PASS.** All 200, full OG/Twitter/canonical, compliant footers, illustrative-example disclaimers present.

## Bug list

### CRITICAL

1. **`/sales` shows zero dollar figures while promising "No hidden fees."**
   The pricing section advertises a paid plan ("Grow — One simple plan") with no price
   anywhere on the page (confirmed: 0 `$` in `sales.html` source). The standalone
   `/pricing` page exists with real prices but `/sales` never links to it.
   Evidence: section `<h2 id="plans-heading">Start free. Grow when you're ready.</h2>`
   → plan cards "Free to start" / "One simple plan" / "As you need it" →
   `No hidden fees. No credit card to start. Cancel anytime. Purchases are final — no refunds.`
   This directly fails the product-compliance hidden-fees check (item 9).

### SEVERE

2. **Tier-name mismatch between `/sales` and the product.**
   `/sales` uses `Start` / `Grow` / `Top up`; the app, `/pricing`, and `/terms` all use
   `Free $0` / `Pro $14/mo` and `Small $4` / `Medium $8` / `Large $12` credit packs.
   A buyer reading the sales page cannot map what they read to what checkout bills.
3. **No provider-billing disclosure on marketing pages.**
   `/sales` claims "Every unanswered call gets an instant text-back" and
   "Text-back works once you connect your business number — setup takes minutes" —
   with no mention that the number rental, SMS, and call minutes run on the buyer's
   OWN Twilio/Telnyx account, billed by that provider. Confirmed by source grep:
   no marketing page (`sales.html`, `pricing.html`, any vertical page) mentions
   Twilio, Telnyx, or provider billing. The disclosure exists only on `/terms`
   ("Phone-provider usage is billed separately by your provider") and in the app FAQ
   ("Phone features require a supported provider, a connected number, and completed setup").
4. **`/sales` has no link to `/pricing`.**
   The header nav "Pricing" jumps to the internal `#plans` anchor (which shows no prices).
   The one page with dollar figures is unreachable from the flagship sales page.
5. **Vertical pages are orphaned.**
   The seven money-loop trade cards on `/sales` contain ZERO `<a>` tags (verified in
   source: 0 links inside `loop-grid`). Users and crawlers cannot reach any
   `/sales/<slug>` page from `/sales`.

### MINOR

6. **Short-slug vertical URLs 404:** `/sales/plumbing` → 404, `/sales/auto` → 404.
   Canonical slugs (`/sales/plumbing-hvac`, `/sales/auto-repair`) work. Consider redirects.
7. **`/pricing` lacks Open Graph/Twitter meta tags** (`sales.html` and all vertical
   pages carry full OG/Twitter/canonical sets).
8. **`/` (index.html) lacks `rel=canonical`.**
9. **`/get-started` is a contentless SPA fallback** — 200, no 404, but no dedicated
   content and no CTA routes to it.

## Non-issues (checked, cleared)

- **"Homepage says five trades, sales pages imply seven" — NOT REPRODUCED.** The
  app landing page states no trade count. `/sales` says "One loop, seven trades,"
  which exactly matches the seven trade cards and the seven runtime vertical
  profiles. No "five trades" text exists in any marketing source.
- **"Pet-grooming / med-spa exceed the 5 runtime profiles" — NOT REPRODUCED.**
  `workers/mayor/src/verticals.ts` defines full profiles for all seven trades
  (salon, restaurant, plumbing_hvac, dental, auto_repair, pet_grooming, med_spa)
  plus `other` — each with vocabulary, KPIs, and onboarding questions
  (e.g. pet_grooming: rebooking_rate / table_utilization / avg_ticket;
  med_spa: rebooking_rate / room_utilization / avg_ticket / no_show_rate).
  The 7 sales pages match the 7 runtime profiles 1:1. (The vision doc aspires to
  "40+ vertical agent definitions" as a roadmap note, not a shipped state.)
- **Illustrative-example disclaimers — PRESENT.** All 7 trade cards carry
  "Example · Illustrative — results vary" badges and the section lead states
  "not a customer story. Results vary; nothing here promises revenue." The FAQ
  repeats: "The examples on this page are illustrative walkthroughs … not promises."
