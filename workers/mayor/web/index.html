    A HAND WHEN YOU NEED IT

    Help

    The essentials for a clearer workday.

    How do I get started?

    Sign in, tell the Assistant about your business, and confirm your profile. Connect a calendar in Connections, then confirm your services, opening hours, and booking rules with the Assistant.

    What appears in my appointments?

    This workspace shows appointments created through The Mayor. Your calendar can contain other events; The Mayor checks those when booking. Choose the business date to review a different day.

    Can the assistant contact my customers?

    You can ask for a message draft and copy it to your own messaging app. Messages are not sent from this workspace. Phone features require a supported provider, a connected number, and completed setup.

    How does voice work?

    Choose Talk, allow microphone access, and speak naturally. You can interrupt, mute, or end the conversation. If voice cannot start, your typed messages still work. Phone calls and provider charges are separate from microphone minutes.

    Can I use this offline or install it?

    Install The Mayor using your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option. Opening the app offline is supported; business actions and conversation require a connection. An unsent draft stays on this page until you leave it.

    Who can change business records?

    Owners and managers can manage customers and appointments. Owners and people with the billing role manage subscriptions. Staff can use the Assistant. Your role appears in Settings and the workspace menu.
