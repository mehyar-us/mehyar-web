    How this works
    How do I get started?

    Sign in, tell The Mayor about your business, and confirm your profile. Connect a calendar in Connectors, then confirm your services, opening hours, and booking rules in conversation.

    What does Today show?

    Your morning briefing, anything needing attention, upcoming Mayor bookings, and next steps. Appointments, customers, and tasks open from here — tap View all, Customers, or View tasks.

    Can The Mayor contact my customers?

    You can ask for a message draft and copy it to your own messaging app. Messages are not sent from this workspace. Phone features require a supported provider, a connected number, and completed setup.

    How does voice work?

    Choose Talk, allow microphone access, and speak naturally. You can interrupt, mute, or end the conversation. If voice cannot start, your typed messages still work.

    Who can change business records?

    Owners and managers can manage customers and appointments. Owners and people with the billing role manage subscriptions. Your role appears in Settings.
