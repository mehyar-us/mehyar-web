A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before you begin.

Is this a subscription?

The business audit is $330 USD as a one-time purchase. The Mayor workspace plans and third-party services are separate.

What if some information isn’t public?

Share relevant context and authorized document links in the brief. The report identifies what was reviewed and clearly marks areas where the available evidence is limited.

Will you make changes to my business?

The audit provides findings and recommendations. Website changes, account changes, customer outreach, and other implementation work need a separate agreement.

Does the audit guarantee more revenue?

The action plan helps you make informed decisions. Impact and effort are estimates; the report includes ways to check results against your business goals.

How is the report generated?

The Mayor gathers the public evidence it can access, combines it with your brief, and uses AI to generate and review the report. Unsupported claims and inaccessible areas must be identified. The saved report appears through your private status page after generation and review complete.

How do I check my request?

Keep the private receipt link shown after checkout. It displays verified payment status and the report’s recorded status. If generation needs attention, the status says so. No delivery deadline is automatically promised.

Audit scope and terms

The $330 USD one-time purchase covers an audit of one business and its primary public website across the six areas above. Findings use available public sources and the information you authorize us to review. The completed report includes annotated evidence, source references, priorities with estimated effort and likely impact, and a 30/60/90-day action plan.

Implementation, ongoing monitoring, third-party subscriptions, advertising spend, private account access, and specialist legal, financial, or security assessments are outside this offer. Share only material you’re authorized to provide. Your private status page shows payment and fulfillment separately; report availability is recorded when the deliverable is ready.
