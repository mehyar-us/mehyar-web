Hair salon / Barbershop

  1. The missed appointment. A regular calls on your day off to move her color appointment. Nobody picks up — normally that chair sits empty and she books somewhere else.
  2. The instant text-back. The Mayor texts back almost instantly, in your voice: Hi, this is your business. Sorry we missed your call! Want to book an appointment? Reply YES and we will find you a time. Reply STOP to opt out.
  3. The recovered appointment. She replies YES. The Mayor follows up with your open chair times, and the rebooked appointment lands on your book before you open the shop.

Do you guarantee more bookings or revenue?

No. The examples on this page are illustrative walkthroughs of the missed-call text-back flow — not customer stories and not promises. The Mayor answers faster and keeps your day organized; results vary by business.

What happens after I sign up?

You sign in, tell The Mayor about your business, and it starts with your real book: missed-call text-back, reminders, and a daily briefing in your language.

Which phone providers does The Mayor work with?

You connect a business number you own through Telnyx or Twilio. Once a Telnyx number is connected, missed-call text-backs are sent automatically from that number; until a number is connected, The Mayor prepares drafts you can copy into your own messaging app. Twilio numbers connect for voice calls; automatic text-backs are sent through Telnyx.
