Free and Pro plans

Free includes 100 assistant reply attempts and 10 reserved app microphone minutes per business per UTC calendar month. Pro costs $14 USD per month and includes 1,000 reply attempts and 120 reserved app microphone minutes per verified paid period, shared by the business. Stripe processes subscriptions and audit purchases. Pro activates after payment is verified, not merely after returning from checkout. A failed, refunded or disputed payment can affect paid access. Contact us about billing errors, refund requests or a failed service delivery; statutory rights continue to apply.
